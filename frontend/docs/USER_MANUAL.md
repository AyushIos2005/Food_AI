# FoodAI — User Manual

**Cook • Create • Share** — an AI-powered food and recipe platform.

This manual is for people using FoodAI with a **User** account. If you signed up as a
**Chef**, read the Admin/Chef Manual too — chefs can do everything here plus publish recipes.

---

## 1. Getting started

### Create an account
1. Open FoodAI and press **Get Started**.
2. Pick your account type at the top of the form:
   - **User** — cook, browse recipes, post in the community.
   - **Chef** — everything above, plus publishing your own recipes.
3. Fill in username, full name, email and password (minimum 6 characters), then confirm it.
4. Press **Create account**.

> Your username may only contain letters, numbers, dots, hyphens and underscores, and must be
> 3–40 characters long.

### Verify your email
After registering you land on the **Verify your email** screen and a 6-digit OTP arrives in
your inbox. Type or paste it into the boxes and press **Verify Email**.

- The OTP is valid for **10 minutes**.
- You can request a new one after **60 seconds**.
- Check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived.

### Log in
Enter **either** your username **or** your email, plus your password. FoodAI keeps you signed
in with a secure cookie, so you stay logged in until you log out.

### Forgot your password?
1. On the login screen press **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your registered email — an OTP is sent to it.
3. On the next screen enter the OTP and your new password twice.
4. Press **Reset Password**, then log in normally.

## 2. Finding your way around

**On a computer** the left sidebar holds every section. **On a phone** the main sections sit
in the bar at the bottom, with the green AI button in the middle.

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your dashboard — quick actions plus the latest recipes and posts |
| **AI Recipe** | Create a recipe from ingredients, or reinvent a dish you know |
| **Recipes** | Browse and search recipes published by chefs |
| **Community** | The social feed — posts, photos, videos, comments |
| **Saved** | Posts you bookmarked |
| **Notifications** | Likes, comments, follows and system messages |
| **Profile** | Your public details |
| **Settings** | Password, feedback, complaints, contacting the developer |

The **search box** at the top searches recipes. The **bell** shows a red badge with your
number of unread notifications. Your **avatar** at the top right opens a menu with Profile,
Settings and Logout.

## 3. Creating a recipe with AI

### From ingredients you have
1. Go to **AI Recipe → Create New Recipe**.
2. Type each ingredient and press **Enter** (or comma) to add it. Add at least one; up to 30.
3. Set **Number of persons** with the − and + buttons.
4. Optionally add a **medical condition or dietary restriction** — for example *diabetic*,
   *low sodium*, *gluten free*. The AI works around it.
5. Press **Generate Recipe** and wait a few seconds.

You get back a full recipe card: name, description, protein and calories per serving,
servings, prep time, the ingredient list, protein sources, any medical considerations, and
numbered step-by-step instructions.

From the result you can **Generate Another** or jump to **My AI Recipes**.

### Reinventing a dish you already know
1. Go to **AI Recipe → Recreate Existing Food**.
2. Type the dish name — for example *Paneer Tikka*.
3. Add the new ingredients you want worked in.
4. Press **Create New Version**.

You get a new dish built on the original, showing what was added and full instructions.

### Your AI history
**My AI Recipes** lists everything the AI made for you, newest first. You can:

- Filter by **All**, **AI Created** or **Recreated** using the chips at the top.
- Tap any card to reopen the full recipe.
- Press the bin icon on a card to remove it.
- Press **Undo** in the *Recently deleted* box to bring one back.
- Press **Clear all** to empty your history (individual items can still be undone).

> Deletes are soft — nothing is destroyed immediately, which is why Undo works.

**Fair use:** AI generation is rate-limited. If you make a lot of recipes quickly you may be
asked to wait a moment before the next one.

## 4. Browsing chef recipes

Open **Recipes** to see everything chefs have published. Type in the search box to filter by
dish name — results come straight from the server.

Tap **View Recipe** on any card for the full page: large photo, the chef's name, description,
the complete ingredient list, and a **precautions** note (allergens, spice level, and so on).
Read that note before cooking, especially if you have allergies.

## 5. The community

### Reading the feed
**Community** shows posts newest first — the author, how long ago it was posted, the text,
any hashtags, and the photo or video.

Under each post:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| ❤️ Heart | Like / unlike. Tapping again removes your like. |
| 💬 Speech bubble | Open comments |
| ↗️ Share | Records a share and copies the link (or opens your phone's share sheet) |
| 🔖 Bookmark | Save / unsave to your **Saved** list |

### Writing a post
1. Press **+ Post** on the Community screen.
2. Add up to **10** photos or videos (up to 50 MB each — JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4, WEBM, MOV).
   At least one is required.
3. Write your description. Any **#hashtag** you type becomes a clickable tag automatically.
4. Optionally add Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, YouTube or LinkedIn links.
5. Press **Publish**.

> Uploads are rate-limited, so wait a moment between large posts.

### Comments
Open the comment sheet, type at the bottom and press send. You can delete **your own**
comments — and, on your own posts, anyone's comment. Use the small bin icon next to a comment.

### Hashtags
Tap any **#hashtag** to see every post carrying it, with the post count at the top.

### Saved posts
Everything you bookmarked lives under **Saved**. Tap the bookmark again to remove it.

### Deleting your own post
Press the bin icon in the post header. You can only delete posts you created. This cannot be
undone.

## 6. Notifications

The bell in the top bar carries a red badge with your unread count, and new notifications
arrive **live** while you have FoodAI open — no refresh needed.

Open **Notifications** to see likes, comments, follows, shares, saves and system messages.
Unread ones have a green tint and a dot. Tapping one marks it read and jumps to the post or
recipe it refers to. **Mark all read** clears the badge in one go.

## 7. Your profile

### Setting it up
The first time you open **Profile** you'll be asked to create one. Press **Create Profile**
and fill in:

- **Full name** (required)
- **Bio** — a short line about your cooking
- **Profession** and **Contact number**
- **Date of birth**
- **Hobbies** — added one at a time like ingredients
- **Social media links** — paste full URLs

Press **Create Profile** to save. Use **Edit** on the profile card to change anything later.

Your profile also shows your **follower** and **following** counts.

## 8. Settings

Reach these from the sidebar, or your avatar menu → Settings.

### Security
- **Change password** — enter your current password, then the new one twice. The new
  password must be at least 6 characters.
- **Logout** — signs you out on this device.

### Feedback
Rate FoodAI from 1 to 5 stars and press **Submit Rating**. For anything you want to describe
in words, use Complaint instead.

### Complaint
Describe an issue in up to 2000 characters and press **Submit Complaint**.

> This is available to **User** accounts only. Chef accounts should use Contact Developer.

### Contact Developer
Send the developer a full message — name, address, contact number, email and your reason.
Your name and email are pre-filled from your account. It arrives as an email.

## 9. Troubleshooting

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Please login first" / sent back to login | Your session expired | Log in again |
| "Network error — check your connection" | The app can't reach the server | Check your internet; confirm the backend is running |
| "You are not authorized to do that" | The action needs a different role | Chef-only or user-only feature — see the manuals |
| "That already exists" on register | Username or email is taken | Try a different one, or log in |
| "That code didn't work" | Wrong or expired OTP | Request a new one; codes last 10 minutes |
| "Please wait before requesting another OTP" | Sent too soon | Wait 60 seconds |
| Recipe generation fails | AI service busy or rate-limited | Wait a moment and try again |
| Photo won't upload | File too large or wrong type | Max 50 MB; JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4, WEBM, MOV |
| Notifications don't arrive live | Socket connection blocked | Reload the page; the list still updates when you open it |

---

## 10. Privacy and safety

- Your password is stored **hashed** — nobody, including the developer, can read it.
- Your session lives in a secure **httpOnly cookie** that JavaScript on other sites cannot read.
- Logging out **blacklists** your session token immediately, so it can't be reused.
- Anything you post in the community is visible to all logged-in FoodAI users.
- AI recipes are suggestions, not medical advice. Check ingredients yourself if you have
  allergies or a medical condition.
